⚡ AI expands inside Excel ↗

Claude is now available in Excel for paid plans and can build complex multi-tab spreadsheets. It runs on a newer model than Copilot today, with Microsoft planning to upgrade Copilot to the same model in about 60 days. The inital tests I did on this were very impressive, where it was able to go out and track down data on the internet, put it in a table, create a pivot table, and create several graphs and charts.

⚡ Health and education AI accelerate ↗

Health-focused AI tools are integrating sources like ICD-10 and PubMed while major education initiatives from Anthropic and OpenAI plan to train educators and support national systems. Right now these tolls are available in Claude. ChatGPT is rolling out the health GPT very soon. ChatGPT recently noted that the most common use case they have is mental healt and relationship advice.

⚡ Privacy pressure increases ↗

AI wearables and medical analysis tools are increasing concerns around continuous data collection and privacy. OpenAI has a wearable device coming this year that is rumored to be earbuds or something that is pen-shaped. In many ways the privacy ship has sailed with regard to our data, but this will push that envelope a bit further.

Sources: macrumors.com ↗ · helpnetsecurity.com ↗

📰 Ads introduced in ChatGPT ↗

Free users will begin seeing targeted ads inside the chat interface. The ads will be only on free accounts, and will be at the bottom of the page. Early indications are that the adds will be based upon the context that OpenAI understands about you from your chat history. The claim is that ads will not be like search engine ads, but rather more focused on what you would like/need.

🔬 Copilot as a productivity multiplier ↗

AI embedded in office tools are highly dependent upon the quality of the data that we are providing. For example, the powerpoint for this weeks meeting was generated by AI, however, it's not very elegant because the data I gave it was not well-structured. The key to these tools being as helpful as possible is to have the data formatted the way it will best be understood. This is a lot of trial and error, I believe.

🎓 Large-scale AI education initiatives ↗

Anthropic and OpenAI are investing heavily in educator training and broader education support. Anthropic launched Claude for Education, a version of Claude tailored for universities, with features like “Learning mode” that use Socratic questioning to guide students instead of dumping answers. OpenAI has an education line built around ChatGPT Edu and other education-focused tools, noting that a large share of college‑age users already use ChatGPT for learning

🎓 AI-assisted research skills

Generative AI could significantly enrich student learning, but its current use poses serious cognitive, emotional, social, and equity risks that must be actively managed. Benefits such as personalized learning pathways, rapid feedback, better assessment design, and expanded access—especially for under-resourced schools, students with disabilities, neurodivergent, and multilingual learners are possible.
