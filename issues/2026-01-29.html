Highlights

Claude and Copilot upgrades for Excel

Claude is now available in Excel for paid plans and can build complex multi-tab spreadsheets. Microsoft plans to upgrade Copilot to the same model level within ~60 days.

Health and education initiatives are accelerating

Claude, ChatGPT, and Amazon are rolling out health-focused extensions with strong diagnostic capabilities, while Anthropic and OpenAI are launching large-scale education programs.

New consumer features + practical demos

Ads, shopping assistants, and age prediction are coming to ChatGPT and Perplexity. We also demoed translation, recurring reminders, news summaries, and advanced video/math analysis.

News

Ads and shopping arrive in ChatGPT and Perplexity

Free ChatGPT users will see targeted ads based on usage history, and both tools now support shopping workflows directly inside chat.

Age prediction for content guardrails

OpenAI is rolling out age prediction to limit adult content for users identified as under 18. Use caution when sharing screens with ChatGPT outputs.

New Tools

Claude in Excel (paid plans)

Can generate complex spreadsheets across multiple tabs and boost common business workflows.

Polyglotta translation

Multilingual translation tool reported as a strong alternative to Google Translate for academics.

Gemini video and math analysis

Handles video breakdowns and step-by-step math reasoning with graphical support.

Copilot email summarization

Summarizes long threads into prioritized bullets and highlights implicit insights.

Analysis

Privacy risks rising with health AI and wearables

Uploading sensitive data for analysis is becoming more common. OpenAI and Apple wearable roadmaps include continuous data capture and facial recognition, so privacy policies matter.

Automation opportunities for repetitive tasks

Share time-consuming tasks to explore automation with current AI tools and workflows.

Education Impact

Teach for All and Education for Countries

Anthropic aims to train 100,000 educators, while OpenAI is targeting national education systems with curriculum, tooling, and support programs.

AI-assisted statistical validation

Multiple tools (Julius AI, ChatGPT, and a statistician) produced consistent results for dissertation analysis, showing promise for research workflows.

Demonstrations

Translation, reminders, and news summaries

ChatGPT can generate recurring weather and news summaries and deliver them via email, calendar, or messaging tools like Slack and Teams.

Video analysis with Gemini

Short sports clips can be analyzed for technical feedback when prompts specify what to assess.
