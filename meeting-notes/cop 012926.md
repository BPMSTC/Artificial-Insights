The Breakdown:
- Title: Copilot as a productivity multiplier
  Summary: AI embedded in office tools are highly dependent upon the quality of the data that we are providing. The key to these tools being as helpful as possible is to have the data formatted the way it will best be understood.  This is a lot of trial and error, I believe.
