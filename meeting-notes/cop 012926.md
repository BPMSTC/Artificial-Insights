- Title: Copilot as a productivity multiplier
  Summary: AI embedded in office tools are highly dependent upon the quality of the data that we are providing.   For example, the powerpoint for this weeks meeting was generated by AI, however, it's not very elegant because the data I gave it was not well-structured.  The key to these tools being as helpful as possible is to have the data formatted the way it will best be understood.  This is a lot of trial and error, I believe.
